Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

The Gadget Galley ("we", "us") operates this website and the Pinterest application "Gadget Galley Publisher". This policy explains what data we handle and how.

Website visitors

This website collects no personal information. We do not use cookies, analytics trackers, or advertising pixels. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested) for security and operational purposes.

Pinterest application

"Gadget Galley Publisher" is a single-account tool used exclusively by us to manage our own Pinterest presence. It:

  • publishes Pins and manages boards on our own Pinterest business account, with our authorization via Pinterest's OAuth flow;
  • reads analytics (impressions, saves, clicks) for our own Pins to inform future content;
  • does not access, collect, or store any data belonging to other Pinterest users;
  • does not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties.

OAuth tokens are stored securely on our own infrastructure and are used solely to operate our own account. You can learn more about Pinterest's own data practices in the Pinterest Privacy Policy.
